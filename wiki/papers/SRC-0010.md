## Abstract & Overview
We explore how generating a chain of thought—a series of intermediate reasoning steps—signiﬁcantly improves the ability of large language models to perform complex reasoning. In particular, we show how such reasoning abilities emerge naturally in sufﬁciently large language models via a simple method called chain-of- thought prompting, where a few chain of thought demonstrations are provided as exemplars in prompting.
