## Abstract & Overview
This chapter addresses the theoretical foundations for research in educational communications and technol­ ogy. Four relevant areas are explored: (1) the psychol­ ogy of learning, (2) communications theory, (3) human–computer interaction, and (4) instructional design and development. Past work in these four areas can be viewed as providing a theoretical foundation for further research and development in educational com­ munications and technology.
