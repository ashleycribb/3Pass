## Abstract & Overview
The rapid advances of large language models (LLMs), such as ChatGPT, are revolutionizing data science and statistics. These state-of-the-art tools can streamline complex processes such as data cleaning, model building, interpretation, and report writing. As a result, it reshapes the role of data scientists. We argue that LLMs are transforming the responsibilities of data scientists, shifting their focus from hands-on coding, data-wrangling and conducting standard analyses to assessing and managing analyses performed by these automated AIs. This evolu- tion of roles is reminiscent of the transition from a software engineer to a product manager, where strategic planning, coordinating resources, and overseeing the overall product life cycle supersede the task of writing code. We illustrate this transition with concrete data science case studies using LLMs in this paper. These developments necessitate a meaningful evolution in data science education.
