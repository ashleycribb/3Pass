## Abstract & Overview
Large language models are increasingly used to power personal agents for everyday appli- cations, but evaluating these agents remains a challenge. Existing benchmarks still rely on sandboxed artifacts, static task design, and coarse scoring, which hinder scalability and limit progress toward reliable personal-agent evaluation. This paper introduces STAGE- Claw, an automated framework for building and evaluating realistic personal-agent scenarios in state-based personal-computing environments. Gi
