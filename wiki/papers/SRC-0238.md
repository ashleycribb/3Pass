## Abstract & Overview
arXiv:2505.24477v1 [cs.CY] 30 May 2025 goo.gle/LearnLM-May25 2025-05-19 Evaluating Gemini in an Arena for Learning LearnLM Team, Google Artificial intelligence (AI) is poised to transform education, but the research community lacks a robust, general benchmark to evaluate AI models for learning. To assess state-of-the-art support for educational use cases, we ran an “arena for learning” where educators and pedagogy experts conduct blind, head-to- head, multi-turn comparisons of leading AI models.
