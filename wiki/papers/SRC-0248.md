## Abstract & Overview
Large language models (LLMs) have emerged as a widely-used tool for information seeking, but their generated outputs are prone to halluci- nation. In this work, our aim is to allow LLMs to generate text with citations, improving their factual correctness and verifiability. Existing work mainly relies on commercial search en- gines and human evaluation, making it chal- lenging to reproduce and compare different modeling approaches.
