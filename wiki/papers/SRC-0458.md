## Abstract & Overview
This report documents the design, rationale, and structure of a novel OWL (Web Ontology Language) Ontology that formally encodes education technology principles to serve as a grounding layer for AI and Agentic AI systems. The ontology directly addresses the "reliability gap" in AI-assisted education—where LLMs produce fabricated citations, logical hallucinations, and methodological misalignments at alarming rates, with some reasoning models exceeding 33–51% error rates on factual benchmarks.
