## Abstract & Overview
Large language models (LLMs) often respond even when prompts omit critical details or in- clude misleading information, leading to hal- lucinations or reinforced misconceptions. We study how to evaluate and improve LLMs’ ability to decide when and what to ask for clarification without sacrificing task perfor- mance. We introduce AskBench, an inter- active benchmark that converts standard QA pairs into multi-turn interactions with explicit checkpoints. A unified judge loop evaluates final answers
