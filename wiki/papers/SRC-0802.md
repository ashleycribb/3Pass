## Abstract & Overview
The integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) into the scientific ecosystem raises fundamental questions about the creativity and originality of AI-generated research. Recent work has identified “smart plagiarism” as a concern in single-step prompting approaches, where models reproduce existing ideas with terminological shifts. This paper investigates whether agentic workflows—multi-step sys- tems employing iterative reasoning, evolu- tionary search, and recursive decomposition— can generate more novel and feasible research plans.
