## Abstract & Overview
Artificial intelligence (AI) has surpassed humans in a number of specialised intellectual activities—chess and Go being two of many examples. Amongst the many potential con- sequences of such a development, I focus on how we can utilise cutting edge AI to pro- mote human learning. The purpose of this article is to explore how a specialised AI can be utilised in a manner that promotes human growth by acting as a tutor to our champions.
