## Abstract & Overview
The proliferation of mobile devices in higher education creates a need for further research to better understand how students are using mobile technology for learning. This explanatory mixed methods research design will advance the research of mobile learning in higher education by specifically focusing on community college students in the United States and by identifying themes of student behavior in online courses with mobile technology, two research areas that are currently lacking.
